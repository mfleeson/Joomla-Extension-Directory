# Maintainers and handing over ownership

Every listing has exactly **one owner** - the account responsible for it - and can have any number
of **maintainers** who help look after it.

| | Owner | Maintainer |
|---|---|---|
| Edit the listing | Yes | Yes |
| Respond to reviews | Yes | Yes |
| Take the listing offline / put it online | Yes | Yes |
| Add or remove maintainers | Yes | Yes (on the listing's Maintainers tab) |
| Hand the listing over to someone else | Yes | No |
| Responsible for the listing | Yes | No - the owner stays responsible |

## Inviting a maintainer

1. Open the listing's edit form (**My Dashboard > My Extensions**, click the name).
2. On the **Maintainers** tab, add a row and pick the user.
3. Choose **Submit**.

The person is **invited**; they get no rights until they accept. You cannot add the owner as a
maintainer (the row is ignored). Changes to the maintainers list take effect as soon as you save
- they are not held for moderation - and are recorded in the JED team's action log.

To **remove** a maintainer, delete their row on the Maintainers tab and save.

## Accepting or declining an invitation

If you have been invited, **My Dashboard** shows a **Maintainer invitations** card at the top:
"You have been invited to help maintain these listings. Accepting lets you edit the listing and
answer its reviews; the owner stays responsible for it."

Choose **Accept** or **Decline** next to the listing. Once accepted, the listing appears in your
**My Extensions** card.

## Handing a listing over to another developer

Use this when someone else is taking over your extension. The listing changes hands only when
**both of you confirm**.

1. Make sure the new owner has an **activated, unblocked account** on the JED.
2. On **My Dashboard**, find the **Handovers** card.
3. Choose the **Extension** and enter the **New owner's account e-mail** - the e-mail address of
   their account on this site.
4. Choose **Hand over**.

"A confirmation request has been sent to *name* and to you." Then:

- **You** receive "Confirm handing over *extension*" with a confirmation link.
- **The new owner** receives "*your name* would like to hand *extension* over to you" with their
  own link.
- Each of you opens your link **while signed in as yourself** (forwarding a link achieves
  nothing).
- When both have confirmed, you both receive "*extension* has changed hands".

While a handover is in progress, the Handovers card shows it with "Waiting for *name* to confirm"
or "Waiting for your confirmation - see the e-mail we sent you". **Call off** cancels it; both
sides are told.

### Rules

- The handover **lapses after 14 days** if not both sides have confirmed; start again if you still
  want it.
- Only one handover can be open per listing at a time.
- You cannot hand a listing to yourself, and only the owner can hand a listing over.
- You can look up at most 10 addresses in 24 hours.
- Handovers are never shown by e-mail address - each side sees the other's **name** only.
- A handover is cancelled automatically if the listing is deleted.

### After the handover

- The new owner manages the listing from their dashboard.
- **The previous owner loses all access** to the listing (they do not become a maintainer). If
  you want to keep helping, ask the new owner to invite you as a maintainer.
- If the new owner was a maintainer, they simply become the owner.
- Other maintainers stay.
- Reviews and developer responses written before the handover stay as they are.

## When the owner cannot be reached

If a listing's owner has disappeared, the JED team can reassign it without the owner's
confirmation (a *forced transfer*: both sides are e-mailed "*name* has been reassigned" with the
reason, and any handover in progress is called off). Open a ticket under **Current Listing
Support** explaining the situation. This is often part of an abandonware case - see
[Security, VEL and abandonware](security-vel-and-abandonware.md).
