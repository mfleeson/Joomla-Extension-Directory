# Extension developers: overview

This part of the help is for developers who list extensions on the JED - submitting new
listings, keeping them up to date, answering reviews, working with your organisation, and dealing with
the JED and VEL teams.

Everything you do as a developer starts from **My Dashboard**:

| Dashboard card | What you do there |
|---|---|
| **My Extensions** | See every listing owned by an organisation you belong to and its status; click a name to edit it; **Take offline** / **Put online**; **Submit an extension** |
| **My Reviews** | Reviews of your extensions (highlighted with a red border) as well as reviews you wrote; respond, report |
| **Organisations** | Accept or decline invitations to join an organisation, approve join requests (organisation admins) and open **My Organisations** |
| **Handovers** | Hand a listing to another developer, and follow handovers in progress |
| **My Tickets** | Your conversations with the JED team |

## The life of a listing

```
 Submit ──► Awaiting approval ──► (JED team review) ──► Online  ◄──►  Offline (your dashboard switch)
                  │                      │
                  │                      └──► Not approved ──► you fix it and edit ──► Awaiting approval
                  │
 (trusted developers skip the queue and are approved at once)

 Online ──► you edit ──► your edit waits for review (the live listing does not change until approved)
 Online ──► JED team blocks it (with a public reason) ──► you fix the cause ──► unblocked
 Online ──► deleted (by the JED team) ──► gone from the site
```

Key principles:

1. **Every change is reviewed.** When you save your listing, a new *revision* is stored and the
   JED team is notified. Your public listing keeps showing the previously approved content until
   the JED team approves the new revision. (Developers the JED team marks as **trusted** are
   approved automatically.)
2. **You are told about decisions by e-mail** - approval, rejection (with the reason code and
   notes), and the results of automated checks.
3. **The listing's visibility has several independent parts.** Approval belongs to the JED team,
   the online/offline switch to the developer, blocking to the JED team, and deletion to the JED
   team. See [Listing status](listing-status.md).
