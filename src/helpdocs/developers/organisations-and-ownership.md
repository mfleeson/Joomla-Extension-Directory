# Organisations and handing over ownership

Listings on the JED belong to **organisations** - a company, a team, or just you. Everybody who is
an active **member** of an organisation can look after **all** of its listings; its
**administrators** also decide who belongs to it and can hand its listings to another
organisation. (Until version 4.1 each listing had its own list of maintainers; see
*Coming from maintainers* below.)

| | Member | Administrator | Primary contact |
|---|---|---|---|
| Edit the organisation's listings, submit new ones | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Respond to reviews, take listings offline / online | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Edit the organisation's details, switch requests on/off | No | Yes | Yes |
| Approve or reject membership requests, invite people | No | Yes | Yes |
| Remove members, change roles | No | Yes | Yes |
| Hand a listing over to another organisation | No | Yes | Yes |
| Confirm handovers; named as the listings' owner | No | No | Yes |

Every organisation has exactly one **primary contact**: an administrator who is the accountable
person for its listings. They confirm handovers and receive the JED team's notices.

Each organisation has a **code** like `ORG-7K3P-9QXD`. Share it with people who should ask to
join, and with organisations that want to hand a listing over to you.

## Creating an organisation

- **When you register**: choose *Create my own organisation* and enter its name.
- **Later**: **My Dashboard > My Organisations > Create an organisation**.

You become its administrator and primary contact. If you submit a listing without belonging to
any organisation, a personal one is created for you automatically.

## Joining an organisation

- **When you register**: choose *Ask to join an existing organisation*, then pick it from the list
  or enter its code.
- **Later**: **My Organisations > Ask to join an organisation**.

The organisation's administrators are e-mailed and decide; you are told the outcome. You can have
at most 3 requests waiting at a time, and you can withdraw one. Some organisations do not accept
requests - ask one of their administrators to invite you instead.

## Inviting people (administrators)

On the organisation's page, **Invite a member**: enter the e-mail address of the person's account
on the JED and choose *Member* or *Administrator*. They get an e-mail and join when they **accept**
on **My Organisations** (or the invitations card on **My Dashboard**). Address look-ups are limited
to 10 per 24 hours and recorded.

Also on the organisation's page: approve or reject **membership requests**, **remove** members,
**make administrator / make member**, and - for the primary contact - **make primary contact** to
hand that role to another administrator.

### Rules

- The primary contact cannot leave, be removed or stop being an administrator; they hand the role
  on first.
- The last administrator of an organisation that owns listings cannot leave.
- Switching membership requests off stops new requests; those already waiting can still be decided.

## Handing a listing over to another organisation

Use this when someone else is taking over your extension. The listing changes hands only when the
**primary contacts of both organisations confirm**.

1. Ask the receiving organisation for its **code**.
2. On **My Dashboard**, find the **Handovers** card (administrators only).
3. Choose the **Extension** and enter the **receiving organisation's code**.
4. Choose **Hand over**.

Both primary contacts receive a confirmation link, which works only while signed in as that person.
When both have confirmed, the listing moves; everybody is told.

- A handover **lapses after 14 days** if not both sides have confirmed.
- Only one handover can be open per listing at a time.
- Administrators of either organisation can **call off** an open handover.

### After the handover

- **The previous organisation's members lose all access** to the listing.
- The receiving organisation's members can look after it at once.
- Reviews and developer responses written before the handover stay as they are.

## When the owner cannot be reached

If a listing's organisation has disappeared, the JED team can reassign it without confirmation (a
*forced transfer*: both primary contacts are e-mailed the reason, and any handover in progress is
called off). Open a ticket under **Current Listing Support**. This is often part of an abandonware
case - see [Security, VEL and abandonware](security-vel-and-abandonware.md).

## Coming from maintainers

When the JED moved to organisations (version 4.1), every listing owner got a **personal
organisation** owning their listings, with them as administrator and primary contact.

- Somebody who maintained **all** of an owner's listings became a **member** straight away.
- Somebody who maintained only **some** of them appears as a **membership request** on the
  organisation's page - because membership gives access to every listing, the administrator
  decides. Until then they have no access.
- Invitations that covered all of an owner's listings became organisation invitations.
