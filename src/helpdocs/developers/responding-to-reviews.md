# Responding to reviews

As a member of the organisation that owns a listing you can publicly answer each review of it **once**.

## Finding reviews of your extensions

Go to **My Dashboard > My Reviews**. Besides the reviews you wrote yourself, the card lists the
reviews of the extensions you own or maintain; these rows are **highlighted with a red border**.
Click a review's title to open it.

Published reviews also appear on your extension page under **Reviews**.

## Writing a response

1. Open the review (from the dashboard).
2. Under **Your response** you see: "As the developer of this extension you can respond to this
   review once. Your response is checked by the JED team before it is published."
3. Write your response and choose **Submit response**.

Your response is sent to the JED team for moderation ("Your response has been submitted and will
be published once the JED team has checked it."). The review page then shows its status:

| Status shown | Meaning |
|---|---|
| "This response is awaiting the JED team's check and is not yet public." | Pending moderation |
| "This response is published." | Visible under the review on your extension page, headed "Developer response on *date*" |
| "This response was deleted." | Removed - by you or by the JED team |

### Good practice

- Be courteous and factual; thank the reviewer where appropriate.
- Explain what you have done about a problem ("fixed in 2.4.1"), or how to get support.
- Do not argue, reveal private details, or continue a commercial dispute - the JED team will not
  publish such responses.
- You get **one** response per review, and it cannot be edited after submission - write it
  carefully.

## Deleting your response

In **My Dashboard > My Reviews**, choose **Delete response** on the row and confirm. This cannot
be undone, and you cannot respond to that review again afterwards.

## Reporting a review

If a review breaks the review guidelines (for example it is spam, abusive, a support request, a
commercial dispute, or written by someone who has clearly not used the extension), choose
**Report review** on its row in **My Dashboard > My Reviews** (or **Report this review** on the
review page). A ticket is opened for the JED team's review specialists with the review attached.
Explain clearly which guideline is broken.

Please do not report reviews just because they are negative. The JED team will not remove honest
reviews.

## Do not review your own extensions

Developers must not review their own extensions or ask others to post fake reviews. See the
Knowledgebase article *Submitting a Review*.
