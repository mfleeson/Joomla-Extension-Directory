# How to restore an older version of a listing

Every save of a listing is kept as a revision, so you can put an earlier version back.

Permission: **Approve Listings**.

1. **Components > JED > Extensions**, click the listing's **History** icon and open the full
   history page (or go to the listing's history directly).
2. Tick the revision you want to restore (tick two to compare them with each other).
3. Choose **Compare selected**. With one revision ticked, the page compares the **live** listing
   (left) with that revision (right); differences are highlighted.
4. Check it is the right content and choose **Approve**. That revision becomes the live listing.

Notes:

- The **activate** button in the history pop-up only marks which revision is the active working
  copy; it does not change what the public sees.
- Restoring does not change the block, delete or online/offline state, or the Parent Confirmed
  flag - those always stay as they are on the live listing.
- Approving sends the developer the approval e-mail ("Your changes to ... have been approved"); tell
  them why you restored the version.
- **Reject** is not offered for an older revision - only for the one awaiting a decision.
