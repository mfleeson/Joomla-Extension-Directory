# How to link an add-on or a free/paid version

Two kinds of link exist between listings. Both are on the **Linked Extensions** tab.

| Field | Meaning | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| **Add-on For** | "This extension extends that product" (e.g. an add-on for VirtueMart) | Anyone may claim it on **their own** listing; it shows there as "Add-on for *...*". It appears on the **parent's** page (under "Add-ons for this extension") only once the JED team sets **Parent Confirmed** to *Yes*. |
| **Free/Paid Version** | The free and paid editions of the same product, listed separately | Symmetric - both listings show each other under "Also available as". A developer may only set it when they own or maintain **both** listings; otherwise only the JED team may. |

Both fields accept the other listing's JED URL, alias or id.

## Confirming an add-on

1. Open the add-on's listing in **Components > JED > Extensions**.
2. On **Linked Extensions**, check **Add-on For** points at the right parent and set
   **Parent Confirmed** to *Yes*.
3. Save. Parent Confirmed is the JED team's decision and is never changed by approving a
   developer's revision.

Only confirm when the add-on genuinely extends the parent - appearing on a popular product's page
brings traffic, which is why it needs confirmation.

## Linking free and paid editions of different owners

If the two editions belong to different accounts (for example a company and one of its staff),
set **Free/Paid Version** yourself on one of the listings, save and approve the revision.
