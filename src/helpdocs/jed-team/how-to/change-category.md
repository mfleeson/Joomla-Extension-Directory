# How to change a listing's category

1. **Components > JED > Extensions**, open the listing.
2. On **Common**:
   - **Category ID** is the main category: it decides the breadcrumb and the category page the
     listing is browsed under.
   - **Categories** holds any additional categories the listing should also appear in.
3. **Save & Close**, then approve the pending revision (Pending icon > Approve) - the main category
   goes live on approval. Changes to the additional categories take effect as soon as you save.

## Managing the category tree

**Components > JED > Categories** is Joomla's standard category manager for the JED. Create, nest,
rename, publish/unpublish and reorder categories there. The public category list only shows
published categories and orders them by how many published extensions they contain.

Before deleting a category, move its listings to another one.
