# How to manage maintainers

Maintainers can edit a listing and answer its reviews; the owner stays responsible. Normally the
owner manages them from the listing's **Maintainers** tab on the site.

## Adding or removing a maintainer as the JED team

1. **Components > JED > Extensions**, open the listing.
2. On the **Maintainers** tab, add a row and choose the user - or delete a row to remove someone.
3. **Save** (or Save & Close).

Maintainer changes take effect when you save - they do not wait for the revision to be approved
(approve any other changes you made as usual).

- A person you add is **invited**: they must choose **Accept** in the *Maintainer invitations* card
  on their dashboard before they get any rights.
- The owner cannot also be a maintainer; adding them is silently ignored.
- Every addition and removal is recorded in the **User Actions Log**, one entry per person.

## Checking who maintains a listing

Open the listing's **Maintainers** tab. The ticket screen's **Ticket Help** tab also lists a
ticket creator's listings and their role on each.
