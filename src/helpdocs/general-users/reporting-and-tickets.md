# Reporting a problem and support tickets

The JED has a support ticket system that the JED team works from. You need to be signed in to
open a ticket.

## Which route to use

| Your problem | Use |
|---|---|
| You need help **using** an extension, or found a bug in it | Contact the **developer** (Support button on the extension page). The JED team cannot help with this. |
| A listing breaks the JED rules, has broken links, wrong information, copies someone else's work... | **Report this extension** on the extension page |
| A review is spam, abusive or breaks the review guidelines | **Report this review** on the review page |
| A **security vulnerability** in an extension | The VEL form - see [Reporting a vulnerability](reporting-a-vulnerability.md). Never post security details in a public ticket. |
| An extension seems **no longer maintained** | The abandoned form - see [Abandoned extensions](abandoned-extensions.md) |
| A problem with the JED website itself, your listing, or anything else for the JED team | **Create a ticket** (My Dashboard > My Tickets, or the *New Ticket* page) |

## Reporting an extension or a review

1. Open the extension page and choose **Report this extension**, or open the review and choose
   **Report this review**.
2. The ticket form opens already linked to that extension or review. The subject is filled in for
   you ("Reporting Extension - *name*" or "Reporting Review - *title*") and the ticket type is set
   to *Reported Extension* or *Reported Review*.
3. Explain the problem in **Text**. Be specific - what is wrong and where.
4. Choose **Submit**.

## Opening a general ticket

1. Go to **My Dashboard** and choose **Create a ticket** (or open the *New Ticket* page).
2. Choose the **Ticket Type** that fits best, for example:
   - *New Listing Support* - about a listing you submitted that is not yet published
   - *Current Listing Support* - about one of your published listings
   - *Unpublished Support* - about one of your listings that was taken offline
   - *Site Technical Issues* / *Joomla Site Issue* - a problem with the JED website
   - *Reported Extension* / *Reported Review* - see above
3. Enter a **Subject** and describe the issue in **Text**.
4. Choose **Submit**.

## What happens next

- You receive a **Confirmation of Submission** e-mail, and the same text appears as the first
  message in your ticket.
- A JED team member reviews the ticket. Replies from the team are e-mailed to you and appear in
  the ticket's **Message History**.
- The ticket's status tells you where it stands:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | Not looked at yet |
| **Awaiting User** | The JED team has asked you something - please reply |
| **Awaiting JED** | Waiting for the JED team |
| **Updated** | Something changed on the ticket |
| **Resolved** | The team considers the matter dealt with |
| **Closed** | The ticket is closed |

## Replying to a ticket

1. Open **My Dashboard > My Tickets** and click the ticket subject.
2. The ticket shows the **Details** of the item it is about (for example the extension or the
   review), the **Message History**, and a **Reply?** box.
3. Write your message and choose **Submit**.

You can only see your own tickets.
