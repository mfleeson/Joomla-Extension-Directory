# General users: getting started

This part of the help is for anyone using the JED to find extensions, review the ones they use,
report a problem, or check whether an extension is vulnerable or abandoned. You do not need to
be a developer.

## What you can do without an account

- Browse and search the directory, read extension pages and reviews.
- Download extensions (downloads are hosted by each developer, not by the JED).
- Read the [Vulnerable Extensions List](vulnerable-extensions-list.md), its feeds and its API.
- Read the [Abandoned Extensions list](abandoned-extensions.md).
- [Report a vulnerability](reporting-a-vulnerability.md) - guests may use that form if the site
  allows it (it does by default); you confirm your e-mail address first.

## What needs an account

| Action | Where |
|---|---|
| Write, edit or delete a review | *Write a review* button on an extension page - see [Writing reviews](writing-reviews.md) |
| Save favourites | The heart/bookmark button on extension cards and pages - see [Dashboard and favourites](dashboard-and-favourites.md) |
| Report an extension or a review, open a support ticket | *Report this extension*, *Report this review*, or *Create a ticket* - see [Reporting and tickets](reporting-and-tickets.md) |
| Report an abandoned extension | The *Report an Abandoned Extension* form - see [Abandoned extensions](abandoned-extensions.md) |
| See your reviews, favourites and tickets in one place | *My Dashboard* |

## Creating an account and signing in

Use **Register** in the main menu to create an account and **Login** to sign in. Both menu
items are only shown to visitors who are not signed in.

### Signing in with the Joomla Identity Portal

The JED can be set up to let you sign in with your **Joomla Identity Portal** account (the
single sign-on used across joomla.org sites). The first time you do this, a new, empty JED
account is created for you.

**If you already had a JED account before**, you can move your portal sign-in onto it and keep
your extensions, reviews, favourites and tickets:

1. Sign in with the Joomla Identity Portal.
2. Open the **Claim Your Old JED Account** page.
3. Enter the **username or e-mail address of your old account** and choose
   **Send Confirmation Link**.
4. The link is sent to the e-mail address stored **on the old account** (not to your portal
   address) - you need access to that mailbox.
5. Open the link **while you are still signed in with your portal account**. The link is valid
   for **24 hours**.
6. When it succeeds you are asked to sign in again; you now continue with your old account.

Good to know:

- You can only ask for a new link about once a minute.
- The page always gives the same answer whether or not an account matched, so it cannot be used
  to find out who has an account.
- If your new portal account already has its own extensions, reviews, favourites or tickets it
  cannot be merged - contact the JED team.
- Only an account that was created by signing in with the Identity Portal can claim an old one.

## Your privacy

The site uses Joomla's standard privacy tools. If you want a copy of the data held about you, or
want it removed, use the site's privacy request form (*Privacy > Request*). Some records are kept
for a limited time only - for example, the internet address a review was submitted from is
cleared automatically after a set number of days.
