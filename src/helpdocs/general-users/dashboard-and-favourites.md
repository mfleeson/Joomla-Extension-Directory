# Your dashboard and favourites

**My Dashboard** is your personal page on the JED. You need to be signed in; if you are not, you
are sent to the login page.

## What the dashboard shows

The dashboard is a set of cards. Some only appear when there is something in them.

| Card | What it contains |
|---|---|
| **Handovers** | Only for developers - ownership transfers in progress. See [Maintainers and ownership](../developers/maintainers-and-ownership.md). |
| **Maintainer invitations** | Only when someone has invited you to help maintain their listing. Accept or Decline. |
| **My Reviews** | The reviews you wrote - and, if you are a developer, the reviews of **your** extensions (highlighted with a red border). Columns: extension, review title, overall score, date, status (Published / Unpublished / Trashed) and actions. |
| **My Favourites** | Extensions you bookmarked, with their category and the date you added them. |
| **My Extensions** | Only relevant for developers - your listings with their status, and **Take offline** / **Put online** for approved ones. **Submit an extension** starts a new listing. |
| **My Tickets** | Your support tickets with type, subject, date and status. **Create a ticket** opens a new one. |

Each list is paged if it is long.

### Actions on your reviews

- Click the review title to open it.
- **Delete review** removes your review (you are asked to confirm; it cannot be undone).

See [Writing reviews](writing-reviews.md) for editing.

## Favourites

Favourites are your personal bookmarks. Nobody else sees them.

- **Add** - click the favourite button next to an extension's name on its page, or on its card in
  any list. The button is only shown when you are signed in.
- **Remove** - click the button again, or use **Remove** in **My Dashboard > My Favourites**
  (you are asked to confirm).

## Tickets

**My Tickets** lists every support ticket you opened. Click a subject to open the ticket, read
the replies from the JED team and add a reply of your own. See
[Reporting and tickets](reporting-and-tickets.md).
