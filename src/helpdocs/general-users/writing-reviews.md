# Writing and managing reviews

Reviews help other Joomla users choose extensions. You can write **one review per extension**,
and you can edit or delete it later.

## Before you write

You **must have used the extension** before reviewing it. Reviews are checked by the JED team
and only those that follow the guidelines are published.

**Allowed and encouraged**

- A full evaluation of the extension
- Courtesy and honesty
- Pros and cons of the extension and its performance
- The level of support you received
- Ease of use and deployment
- What you used it for (e.g. "I used this extension for a magazine site")
- Level of difficulty
- Your experience level with Joomla and web technologies

**Not allowed**

- Commercial disputes
- Questions to the developer or others
- Support or feature requests
- Error messages
- Links
- Code or bug reports
- Questionable language (including cursing)
- Submissions via an IP-masking service or VPN
- One-line reviews
- Self-promotion or spam

If you are having a **problem** with the extension, contact the developer instead - the review
form shows their e-mail address and website. If you need to report the **listing** itself, use
the **Report this extension** button on the listing, not a review.

The Knowledgebase articles *Submitting a Review*, *Review Scoring Overview* and *Edited, Removed
or Unpublished Reviews* give the full rules.

## Writing a review

1. Sign in.
2. Open the extension page and choose **Write a review**.
3. Read the guidelines and choose **Continue**. The form appears.
4. Fill in the form:

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Title** | Yes | A short summary of your review |
| **General Comments** | | Your overall experience |
| **Functionality** (stars) + comment | Yes | Does it do what it claims? |
| **Ease Of Use** (stars) + comment | Yes | How easy was it to set up and use? |
| **Support** (stars) + comment | | Leave unrated if you never needed support |
| **Documentation** (stars) + comment | | |
| **Value For Money** (stars) + comment | | Mainly for paid extensions |
| **I use this for** | Yes | What you use the extension for |
| **Which version are you reviewing?** | Yes | The version number you used |

5. Choose **Submit**.

Your review is **not public yet**. It goes to the JED team for moderation and you are e-mailed
when it is published ("Your review has been published") or if it is not ("Your review was not
published", with the reason). Until then only you and the JED team can see it - the review page
tells you "This review is not published. Only you and the JED team can see it." Your own
pending review is also shown to you (and only you) among the reviews on the extension page, so
you can see it has not been lost.

When a review is published the extension's scores are recalculated.

## Editing your review

Choose **Edit my review** on the extension page (the button replaces *Write a review* once you
have reviewed that extension), or open the review from **My Dashboard > My Reviews** and choose
**Edit**.

> Editing a published review sends it back for moderation: it is hidden until the JED team
> publishes it again.

## Deleting your review

- From **My Dashboard > My Reviews**, choose **Delete review** on the row; or
- open the review page and choose **Delete**.

Deleting cannot be undone.

## Developer responses

The developer of the extension may answer your review **once**. Their answer is checked by the
JED team before it appears under your review on the extension page.

## Reporting someone else's review

If a review breaks the guidelines (spam, abuse, not about the extension...), open it and choose
**Report this review** (you must be signed in, and cannot report your own). This opens a support
ticket for the JED team with the review attached. See [Reporting and tickets](reporting-and-tickets.md).

## If you cannot review

You may see one of these messages:

- *"You need to be logged in to write a review"* - sign in first.
- *"Your account is not permitted to write reviews"* or *"...to review this developer or this
  category"* - the JED team has restricted your account. If a reason was given it is shown.
- *"Your account is currently suspended..."* - your account is temporarily banned. The message
  includes the reason if there is one.

Contact the JED team through a support ticket if you think this is a mistake.
