If someone else is going to look after your extension - a new maintainer, a company that has bought it - you can hand its listing over to them. The listing keeps its reviews and history; only the owner changes.

How it works

  1. On your dashboard, choose to hand the listing over and enter the email address of the new owner. They need an activated account on the JED.
  2. You and the new owner both receive an email with a confirmation link.
  3. Each of you opens the link while logged in with your own account. A link that has been forwarded to someone else does not work.
  4. When both of you have confirmed, the listing moves to the new owner.

Good to know

  • A request expires after 14 days if it has not been confirmed by both of you.
  • A listing can only have one open request at a time. Cancel the open one to start a new one.
  • If you want someone to help you look after the listing rather than take it over, invite them as a maintainer instead.
