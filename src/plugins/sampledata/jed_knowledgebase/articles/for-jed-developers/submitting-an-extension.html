Getting listed in the JED is a simple process. To start, click here to register or click on the "Register" button at the top of the JED.

Fill out and submit the registration form and you'll be ready to submit extensions. You'll receive an email confirmation and you'll be redy to submit a listing.

To submit a listing, open your profile page or click on the "Profile" button at the top of the JED. Next, click on the "Submit extension" button.

Before submitting a listing, review all of the Submission Requirements. Complete the submission form in its entierty. You'll spend some time completing the form. It's important to enter all required information accurately. When you're done, click on the "Save" button and if the form was completed, you'll receive a notice that your listing is pending. The listing will then be added to the queue for the JED Team to review. You'll be notified with the listing is published or if there are any issues with the listing.
