One of the best parts about Joomla is its extensibility. There are thousands of extensions available in the Joomla Extensions Directory (JED) to help you do just about anything you can imagine with your Joomla site. In the JED you can find, download, rate, favorite and comment on all the extensions the community has to offer. There are some helpful links below to help you get started.
